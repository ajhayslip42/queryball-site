Open with the question the decks couldn't answer at a glance. Keep paragraphs short — most readers are on a phone.

## A heading for the first section

Plain paragraphs work. So does *emphasis*, **bold**, and `code` for a slicer name.

- Bullet lists render as lists.
- Keep each item to a line or two.

1. Numbered lists work the same way.
2. Use them for steps.

> A blockquote for the one sentence you want quoted on Twitter.

## Link back into the deck

Every piece should end with a link into the slice it came from, so the reader can poke at it:
[See it in the Single Player deck](/decks/single-player?sc=lose1to3,lose4to8,lose9to16,lose17&qtr=4)

The link is just the URL from the deck after you set the filters — copy it from the address bar.
